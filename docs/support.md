# Get help

1. **Check the help pages**, especially [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting). Sending `list` shows what Deck Director can see, and `ping` says whether it's listening.
2. **Ask a question** in [Discussions › Q&A](https://github.com/chriskent/deck-director-releases/discussions/categories/q-a): setting up Hangar Control or another app, choosing messages, adopting profiles. Answered questions stay there for the next person.
3. **Report a bug** with a [bug report](https://github.com/chriskent/deck-director-releases/issues/new?template=bug.yml) when something doesn't work as these pages say. It asks for the `ping` reply (which includes your version) and what you sent and got back.
4. **Suggest a feature** with a [feature request](https://github.com/chriskent/deck-director-releases/issues/new?template=feature.yml), or talk an idea through in [Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/chriskent/deck-director-releases/discussions/categories/ideas).

New releases are announced in [Discussions › Announcements](https://github.com/chriskent/deck-director-releases/discussions/categories/announcements). Click **Watch › Custom › Releases** at the top of the [repository](https://github.com/chriskent/deck-director-releases) to be emailed when one comes out.

{: .warning }
Discussions and Issues are public, and need a free GitHub account. Deck Director's log contains your profile names and Windows paths, which may include your user name: check what you paste.
